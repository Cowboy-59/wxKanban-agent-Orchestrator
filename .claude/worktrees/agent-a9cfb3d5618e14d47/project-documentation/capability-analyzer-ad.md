# Stop building the same thing five times.

### Introducing the wxKanban **Portfolio Capability Analyzer** 🧠🔍

---

You've got five apps.

One of them invoices. *Two* of them kind of invoice. Nobody's totally sure which one is the
"real" one. Each talks to the database its own way, in its own dialect, with its own bug you fixed
in app #3 but not app #4. Every app is an island — and your team is paying island taxes.

**What if your whole company could see itself as one system?**

---

## Point it at your apps. Get the truth.

The Capability Analyzer reads across **all** your applications, embeds what each one actually
*does*, and finds the overlap you can't see from inside any single app:

> 🟢 *"Invoicing lives in 3 apps. They're 80% the same."*
> 🟡 *"4 apps re-implement the same customer lookup. Different code. Same job."*
> 🔵 *"This new request? You already built it. It's in the shipping app."*

## Then it answers the only question that matters

For every gap and overlap, one clear recommendation:

| 🏗️ **New app?** | 🔁 **Shared company workflow?** | ➕ **Just a feature in an app you already have?** |
|---|---|---|

No more "let's just build it again, it's easier." No more discovering the duplicate *after* it ships.

---

## Why it's different

- **Semantic, not string-match.** It knows "create invoice" and "generate billing document" are the
  same thing — even when your devs didn't.
- **Always current.** It re-learns every time a scope or app changes. The map never goes stale.
- **It teaches.** Every finding comes with *why it matters* — so your team starts catching the
  duplication before the tool does.
