## HOW THE CROSSING ACTUALLY WORKS

This isn't hand-waving. It's the exact, proven path we used on wxKanban itself — a guided crossing we make *with* you:

1. **Your data comes with you.** We take your proprietary database (HFSQL), generate it to standard SQL, and migrate it cleanly into PostgreSQL, MS SQL, or MySQL — your data exported, transformed, and loaded into a database you own.
2. **Your logic becomes readable.** Your global procedures and window/screen files are converted into clear Markdown — the form AI works with best — so every piece of your application can be analyzed and rebuilt.
3. **Your screens are captured.** Screenshots preserve the exact look of every data screen, so the new application *looks and behaves* like the one your users already know.
4. **AI rebuilds it as modern full-stack.** With your data, logic, and screens prepared, AI does the heavy lifting — and *you* keep the judgment, the control, and the ownership.

No leap of faith. No starting from zero. A guided, step-by-step crossing from proprietary lock-in to software you own.
