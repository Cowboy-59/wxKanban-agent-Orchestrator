## 0. The one thing to remember

> **You're not starting over. You're crossing over — onto ground you own.**

Everything below is in service of that sentence. It is the emotional and strategic
center of the entire campaign. Every headline is a different doorway into it.

---

## 1. Core Promise (the message above all messages)

**Cross to AI-built, full-stack software you actually own — without throwing away a single year of skill.**

- **Who it's for:** the seasoned developer whose livelihood is locked to a proprietary
  platform (beachhead: WINDEV / PCSoft / HFSQL) that could change its terms — or close
  its doors — and take their apps, and their clients' apps, down with it.
- **The tension we resolve:** *"I know I need to move to AI development, but every path
  means abandoning everything I've built."*
- **The promise:** a guided, proven crossing — your data, logic, and screens carried
  across — onto open foundations you own, with the judgment you already have as the asset
  that makes you faster, not obsolete.

**The line that closes it:** *The hard part of software was never the syntax — it was the
judgment. You already have it. AI still demands it. We hand you the new tools and walk you across.*

---

## 2. The Three Pillars

Eleven features collapse into three reasons to believe. Every feature in the synopsis
maps to exactly one pillar. Use these as section headers, ad sets, email themes.

### Pillar I — OWN YOUR FOUNDATION
*"Never bet your career on one vendor's survival again."*

The vendor-independence and conversion story. This is the **emotional lead** — it sells.

- Your data comes with you: HFSQL → standard SQL → PostgreSQL / MS SQL / MySQL you own.
- Your logic becomes readable: procedures + screen files → Markdown AI can rebuild.
- Your screens are captured: the rebuilt app feels familiar from day one.
- Open foundations end-to-end: Express, React, Postgres — yours, not rented.

> *Synopsis sections fed in: 12 (Conversion Bridge — guided service), 1 (Project/Task Mgmt), Tech Foundation.*
> *Honesty guardrail: the crossing is a **guided, done-with-you service & method**, not a one-click in-app converter. Say "we cross it with you," never "click to convert."*

### Pillar II — A LIFECYCLE THAT MAKES YOU BETTER
*"The methodology is the product. It teaches the judgment AI can't."*

The differentiator. This is the **rational proof** — it justifies the choice over generic AI tools.

- The 6-phase lifecycle (Design → Implementation → QA → Human Testing → Beta → Release)
  with stage gates and spec-first discipline.
- An AI orchestrator that scaffolds, validates, fences, and audits — bring your own AI.
- **AI Spec Sessions** — persistent, multi-day AI consultation that develops a spec *with* you (and tracks the time).
- **Scope check-out / check-in in VS Code** — claim a scope, see what's read-only, never collide with a teammate.
- Every release ships a full audit report; compliance is built in, not bolted on.

> *Synopsis sections fed in: 2 (Lifecycle), 3 (AI-Assisted Dev incl. Spec Sessions + scope check-out), 8 (Compliance & Audit), 9 (Docs/Help).*

### Pillar III — RUN THE REAL BUSINESS
*"It's also just a great place to run your projects, your time, and your billing."*

The credibility floor. This is the **table-stakes proof** — it shows wxKanban is a real
product, not a manifesto.

- Tasks, Kanban, notes, and dashboards that sync with Jira, Monday, Asana, Trello.
- Built-in time tracking → billable flagging → PDF invoices.
- Companies, roles, teams, and B2B consulting engagements with seat-aware access.
- **Speaks your users' language** — built-in localization (i18n) with per-user preference.
- **Stack Profiles** — reusable company tech-stack templates that keep teams consistent.

> *Synopsis sections fed in: 1, 4 (PM Integration), 5 (Time/Billing), 6 (Teams), 7 (Consulting), 10 (Platform Admin + lead capture), 11 (Localization + Stack Profiles).*

**Pillar order is deliberate:** lead with **I** (why you'd care), justify with **II**
(why us), reassure with **III** (it's real). Never lead a top-of-funnel asset with Pillar III.

## 3. The Proof (why anyone should believe us)

One proof point outranks all others and should appear in nearly every asset:

> **wxKanban isn't a theory about crossing off a proprietary platform. It *is* one.**
> This product was a WINDEV application. We converted it — data, procedures, screens and
> all — to modern AI-built full-stack. The tool in your hands is the result of the crossing
> we're offering to make with you.

Supporting proof:
- **The founder made every crossing before** (mainframe → web → cloud, 40+ yrs, "Cowboy").
- **The How-To series** — watch the method in the open before committing.
- **You keep ownership and control** — open stack, your database, your code.

## 7. What we do NOT say

- Don't call it "another Kanban board" or lead with features (Pillar III) cold.
- Don't sell syntax, bootcamp, or "learn to code" — the audience are pros.
- Don't promise effortless/automatic conversion — it's *guided*, with judgment kept by the dev.
- Don't imply we replace the developer. AI does the lifting; the human keeps control.
