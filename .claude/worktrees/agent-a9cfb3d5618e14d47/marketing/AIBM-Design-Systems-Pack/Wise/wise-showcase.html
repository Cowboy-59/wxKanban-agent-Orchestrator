Design System
Analysis of Wise

A confident fintech system in Forest Green ink on white, with one electric Bright Green owning every call to action, chunky pill-and-32px rounding, and tight-tracked humanist type that makes money feel calm and borderless.

Typography

Wise Sans (substituted by Inter) — hierarchy built from weight and tight negative tracking. Headings run 600–700; body is a calm 16px / 400 at a 1.5 line-height.

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

An 8px-based scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

Soft and confident — 32px cards and fully-round pill buttons and tags anchor the brand.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. Default; surfaces separated by a hairline, not a shadow.
Level 1 — a soft, low-contrast lift for hovered cards and floating menus.
Level 2 — a deeper diffusion reserved for modals and overlays.
09 — Responsive

Responsive Behavior

NameWidthKey changes
Mobile< 768pxSingle column; pill CTAs stretch wide; display type drops to 32–40px; nav collapses to a menu.
Tablet768–1023pxTwo-column card grids; 24px gutters; section padding tightens.
Laptop1024–1279pxThree-to-four-column feature grids return; content held to a 1200px column.
Desktop≥ 1280pxFull-bleed green / white bands; display type at 48–64px; generous whitespace.

Layout is driven by whitespace and alternating white / Forest-Green bands rather than by visible borders.
