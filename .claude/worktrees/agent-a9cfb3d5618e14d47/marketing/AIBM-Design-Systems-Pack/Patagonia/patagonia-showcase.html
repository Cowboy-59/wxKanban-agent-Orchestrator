Design System
Analysis of Patagonia

A near-monochrome editorial system — white page, true-black ink set airy at 300 weight — where the brand voice lives in pill-shaped buttons and deep slate-and-green outdoor bands.

Typography

Brand face is the proprietary Ridgeway Sans; shown here in Work Sans (nearest Google substitute). The signature move is heavy use of the 300 weight — hierarchy comes from size and negative tracking, not boldness.

03 — Components

Button Variants

White pill with a 2px black border — secondary action, often over photography.

04 — Components

Cards & Surfaces

Image-led product and story cards. Flat at rest (no shadow), lifting to a soft Material-style shadow on hover. White pill badges flag sale or status.

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

A 4px base unit on a 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 scale, with roomy 72px rhythm between sections.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

Two languages: restrained 2–8px corners on surfaces, and fully-rounded 30px pills on interactive controls.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. The resting state for nearly every card.
Level 1 — soft multi-layer shadow on card hover and floating badges.
Level 2 — deep 0 20px 50px shadow reserved for overlays and modals.
09 — Responsive

Responsive Behavior

NameWidthKey changes
Mobile< 768pxCards stack to 1–2 up; pill filters wrap; lede and type ease down modestly.
Tablet768–1023pxProduct / story grids collapse from 4-up to 2-up; gutters tighten.
Laptop1024–1279pxFull 4-up grid returns inside the ~1200px column.
Desktop≥ 1280pxCentered 1200px column with 40px gutters; imagery runs full-bleed.

The system was designed light and roomy, so it stays legible as the column narrows without dramatic breakpoint jumps — type scales gently rather than restructuring.
