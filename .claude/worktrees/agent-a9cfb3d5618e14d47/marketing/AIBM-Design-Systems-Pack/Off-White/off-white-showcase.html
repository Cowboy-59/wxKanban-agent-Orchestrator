Design System
Analysis of Off-White™

A stark gallery-white canvas wired with monospaced IBM Plex type and crisp black hairlines, punctuated by a single electric cobalt blue — industrial, uppercase, and almost entirely square.

Colour Palette

02 — Foundations

Typography

One monospaced family (IBM Plex Mono) sets nearly everything in uppercase; a literary serif — Off-White's proprietary "Bookish", shown here as its nearest Google substitute Spectral — is rationed to prices and status tags.

03 — Components

Button Variants

button-primary
Cobalt #0019e9 fill, white uppercase mono, 2px corners — the community / sign-up action.
button-dark
Pure-black fill: the primary commerce action on every product page.
button-secondary
White with a 1px black rule — the quiet companion to the solid actions.
button-pill
Sunken warm-grey chip for filters and toggles — the one place a softer 20px radius appears.

04 — Components

Cards & Surfaces

Product cards are flat and borderless — a square image, a monospace title, and a serif price. Hover lifts only a tight contact glow.

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

A tight ~4px-based scale; 12px is the canonical field padding and list rhythm.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

Effectively square. 2px dominates by roughly 10×, and nothing on the page exceeds a 20px pill.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. The default; separation comes from rules and whitespace, not shadow.
Level 1 — a tight contact glow on hovered media and floating UI.
Level 2 — detached overlays only: mini-cart and dropdowns.
09 — Responsive

Responsive Behavior

NameWidthKey changes
Mobile< 768pxGrid collapses to 1–2 columns; mega-nav becomes a drawer; 14px mono body holds.
Tablet768–1023pxFour-up product grid drops to two-up; side gutters tighten.
Laptop1024–1279pxFour-up catalog grid; full uppercase navigation bar.
Desktop≥ 1280pxCentered max-width shell with generous gutters; full-bleed editorial media.

Type sizes hold near-constant across breakpoints — the catalog keeps its utilitarian 14px monospace body rather than fluidly scaling, preserving the industrial feel on mobile.
