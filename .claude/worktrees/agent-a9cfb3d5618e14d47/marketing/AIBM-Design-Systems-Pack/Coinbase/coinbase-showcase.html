Design System
Analysis of Coinbase

A crisp, near-monochrome fintech system on white with near-black ink, anchored by the unmistakable Coinbase electric blue on every CTA and link — flat, fully-rounded pill buttons and market green/coral reserved for signal.

02 — Foundations

Typography

Hierarchy is built by size, not weight — display headlines run enormous at a regular weight with tight negative tracking. Coinbase ships in proprietary CoinbaseSans; rendered here in Inter (nearest Google substitute). 16px/400 is the dominant body tier.

04 — Components

Cards & Asset Tiles

Asset tiles sit flat on white with a 1px hairline and 16px radius, lifting to a soft shadow only on hover. Price change uses market green (up) and coral (down).

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

A 4px base unit on an 8-step rhythm. 16px is the default component padding; 24–32px are the common section gaps.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

Generous and consistent: a 4 → 40px ladder topped by a true pill. Coinbase uses the full pill constantly — every button, tab and badge.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. The default. Almost the entire site is flat, bordered surfaces with no shadow.
Level 1 — 0 8px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.12). The rare lifted card, shown on hover of asset tiles.
Level 2 — 0 0 18px rgba(0,0,0,0.2). A diffuse glow reserved for overlays, popovers and modals.

Layout is a centered max-width container with predictable column collapse. Pill buttons and CTAs hold their full-pill shape at every breakpoint.
