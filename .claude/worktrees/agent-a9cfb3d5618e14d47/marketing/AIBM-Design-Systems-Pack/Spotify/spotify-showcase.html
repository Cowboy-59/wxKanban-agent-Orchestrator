Design System
Analysis of Spotify

A confident dark-mode system where near-black canvases let one electric green carry every call to action, white-and-grey geometric type builds the hierarchy, and shapes split cleanly between fully-round pill buttons and small media tiles.

Typography

Set in Spotify Mix / Spotify Circular — a proprietary geometric sans, shown here in its nearest free substitute, Montserrat. Hierarchy is built from size and weight alone: a hard jump from regular 400 body to heavy 700 headings, with letter-spacing left at normal.

03 — Components

Button Variants

button-primary
The green pill with a black label — the single highest-priority action (sign up, play). Brightens and scales ~1.04 on hover.
button-dark
Black pill, white label. Used for app-download prompts and inverted contexts on lighter artwork.
button-secondary
Transparent with a 1px white outline — a lower-priority action that sits beside a primary CTA.
button-pill (utility)
Subtle grey fill for small utility actions in the top bar; brightens on hover.

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

An 8px base unit with a 4px half-step: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48. Controls pad at 8–12px, content gutters at 24–32px.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

A bimodal shape language — controls are fully round (buttons, search field, avatars) while media containers stay gently squared (6–8px).

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. Flat surfaces at rest; most tiles carry no shadow until interacted with.
Level 1 — 0 8px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.3). The softer lift for menus and subtle hover.
Level 2 — 0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.5). The deep card/popover shadow that floats media off the dark canvas.

Responsive Behavior

Laptop1024–1279px5–6 tiles per shelf; the full "Your Library" sidebar returns.
Desktop≥ 1280px6–7+ tiles per shelf at max content width with comfortable 24–32px gutters.

Persistent app shell: a fixed top bar, fixed left sidebar, and fixed now-playing bar bracket a single scrolling column of horizontal media shelves. Album artwork holds a 1:1 aspect ratio at every breakpoint.
