Design System
Analysis of Clay

A warm-paper canvas and crisp black ink anchor a confident, editorial product brand — punctuated by a signature acid-lime pop and a playful set of pastel accent fills.

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

Built on an 8px base — 8 / 16 padding pairs recur throughout — extending to a 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 scale.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

Rounding is generous and pervasive; 18px is the most frequent value, with larger tiers for containers and inputs.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. The default; structure is carried by borders, not shadow.
Level 1 — a single soft drop (0 4px 8px / 15%) used on overlays and menus.
Level 2 — deeper modal/popover shadow (derived).
Signature — a 2px pastel inset ring (here in sky blue) Clay uses in place of conventional shadow.

Responsive Behavior

Note: sections are separated by tone and hairline rules rather than heavy cards, so the layout stays airy as it scales.
