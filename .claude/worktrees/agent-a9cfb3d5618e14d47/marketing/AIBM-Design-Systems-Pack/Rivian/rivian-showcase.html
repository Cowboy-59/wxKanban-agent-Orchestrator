Design System
Analysis of Rivian

A near-black-on-white outdoor-tech system where almost everything is ink and surface — restraint held tight until one electric Rivian Yellow pill earns the click.

Cards & Surfaces

Image-led product cards on white, separated by soft hairlines rather than shadow — depth stays nearly flat, with only a subtle lift on hover.

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

An 8px base unit drives the rhythm — 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 — with generous whitespace between full-bleed media bands.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

Rounded and soft throughout: the full pill is the dominant control shape, with a generous 40px on larger buttons and 20px on cards.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. The default: flat surfaces separated by #d8d8d8 rules and tonal bands.
Level 1 — a subtle 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,.15) lift for floating elements and card hover.
Level 2 — a diffuse lavender-tinted glow on select product surfaces; ambient light, not a drop shadow.

Layout note: the site is editorial and image-forward — stacked full-width feature bands rather than boxed cards, with a centered pill cluster for primary navigation.
