Design System
Analysis of Pitch

A crisp white canvas governed by near-black plum ink, electrified by a single high-voltage violet that carries every CTA — softened by lavender surfaces, a candy-lime pop, and signature coloured glow shadows under big, tightly-tracked geometric display type.

Typography

Display & headings ride proprietary Mark Pro Bold (shown here as Manrope) at 700 with aggressive negative tracking; body & UI use Eina 03 (shown as Inter). Hierarchy is built from size and weight, not colour.

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

A 4px base unit expanding through 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48px, with sections generously padded to let the white breathe.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

Rounding as brand signal: tight 4px on inputs and the primary button, climbing to soft 20–24px cards, 40px feature panels, and a 100px pill.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. Most surfaces sit flat on the white canvas at rest.
Level 1 — soft lavender lift for resting cards: 0 8px 24px -12px rgba(198,165,255,0.30).
Level 2 — the signature coloured glow on hero art & hover: 0 20px 60px -20px rgba(198,165,255,0.35).
09 — Responsive

Responsive Behavior

NameWidthKey changes
Mobile< 768pxSingle column; mega hero type steps down; nav condenses to a menu; large tap targets.
Tablet768–1023pxCard grids collapse 4→2; lede widths tighten; section padding reduces.
Laptop1024–1279pxFull multi-column grids return; display type scales back up.
Desktop≥ 1280pxWide centred max-width column; full 180px hero scale and ambient lavender glow.

Note: the 180px hero scale is desktop-only; the type ramp steps down aggressively below it. The layout consistently favours air and generous rounding over information density.
