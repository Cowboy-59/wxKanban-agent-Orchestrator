Design System
Analysis of On

A monochrome Swiss-performance system where one black ink is text, button, and brand mark on a white canvas — pill-shaped controls, flat hairline surfaces, and a single seafoam-mint accent.

Typography

One proprietary grotesque (substituted here with Hanken Grotesk). Hierarchy comes from size and weight — 400 body, 500 labels and buttons, 700 headings — with tight negative tracking on the display size.

04 — Components

Cards & Surfaces

Product tiles are nearly square and flat — the shoe sits on a soft radial grey backdrop rather than a drop shadow. A thin hairline and whitespace do the separating; hover lifts a whisper-soft shadow.

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

An 8px base with a 4px substep, scaling to unusually generous ~64px page gutters that give the desktop its gallery-like calm.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

Two opposite behaviours: containers stay near-square at 2–4px, while every button and control goes fully round. That square-surface / round-control tension is the signature.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. The default; whitespace and hairlines separate almost everything.
Level 1 — 0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,.1). The gentle lift on a hovered card.
Level 2 — 0 12px 24px rgba(0,0,0,.1). Reserved for the rare modal or popover.
09 — Responsive

Responsive Behavior

NameWidthKey changes
Mobile< 768pxGutters tighten to ~22px; grids fall to 1–2 columns; the split hero stacks.
Tablet768–1023pxFour-up product grids become two-up; the 64px gutters ease in.
Laptop1024–1279pxFull four-column grids return with generous gutters.
Desktop≥ 1280pxEdge-to-edge split hero; max content width with wide 64px gutters.

On's responsive instinct is subtraction — it drops gutters and columns rather than rearranging components, so the calm monochrome character holds at every width.
