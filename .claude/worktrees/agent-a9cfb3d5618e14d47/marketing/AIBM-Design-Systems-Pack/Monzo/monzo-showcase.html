Design System
Analysis of Monzo

A near-white canvas with pure-black ink, generous pill-and-64px roundness, and Monzo's signature hot coral rationed to a single accent — while the real call-to-action is a calm midnight-navy pill.

02 — Foundations

Typography

Monzo's proprietary Monzo Sans family, substituted here with the nearest Google match, Manrope — same rounded geometric construction and an 800 heavy weight. Hierarchy is built from size and weight, not tracking, collapsing to one dominant 16px/400 body tier.

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

An 8px base unit stepping 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 keeps rhythm consistent against the large corner radii.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

Roundness is the signature — from 4px chips up to 64px feature cards and fully-rounded 500px pills.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. The default: surfaces separated by a whisper-light divider, no shadow.
Level 1 — a soft ambient glow (0 0 10px rgba(0,0,0,.1)) on hover for cards and tiles.
Level 2 — a deeper drop (0 8px 32px rgba(0,0,0,.16)) reserved for floating badges and sticky cards.
09 — Responsive

Responsive Behavior

NameWidthKey changes
Mobile< 768pxSingle-column stacks; display headline drops to ~40px; full-width pill CTAs.
Tablet768–1023pxFour-up grids fold to two columns; plan cards stack to one column.
Laptop1024–1279pxFull multi-column grids return; container gains side padding.
Desktop≥ 1280pxCentred 1200px container; alternating white and tinted bands at full width.

The heavy 800-weight display type steps down at the tablet breakpoint so it never overwhelms small screens, while alternating white and mint bands keep a long marketing scroll legible.
