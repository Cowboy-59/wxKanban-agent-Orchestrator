A reverse-engineered style guide for Revolut — a two-mode canvas of true-black storytelling bands and white catalogue bands, oversized Aeonik Pro headlines, pill buttons, and a single cobalt-violet stamp.

01 — Foundations

Colour Palette

Two absolute canvases — white and true black — with a warm near-black ink ramp for text. The cobalt-violet brand colour is rationed; the eight saturated accents live inside illustrations only.

02 — Foundations

The Two-Mode Canvas

Revolut's signature rhythm: full-bleed bands slam between modes rather than fading. The same content reads as a magazine spread.

03 — Foundations

Typography

Aeonik Pro 500 for display (20–136px, tightening as it grows), Inter for body with positive tracking. Display here is rendered in Manrope, the nearest Google substitute for the proprietary Aeonik Pro.

04 — Components

Button Variants

Every button is a full pill. The primary action is the highest-contrast object available — a black pill on white, a white pill on black. Cobalt is used scarcely, never as a default button.

button-dark · primary on light
The loudest action on white catalogue bands — black pill, white label, 48px, full radius.
button-primary · hero on dark
The brightest pixel on a black storytelling band — a white pill inverting the canvas.
button-soft
Tertiary action on light — soft #f4f4f4 fill, ink label.
button-outline · pill-sm
Hairline outline for secondary actions; 36px pill chips for sub-nav and filters.
accent · scarce

05 — Components

Cards & Surfaces

Content cards use a generous 20px radius. On dark bands they lift off true black via surface luminance (#16181a); the featured tier inverts to cobalt.

06 — Components

Form Elements

Inputs run a 12px radius and a tall 56px height — fintech-grade touch targets.

07 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

A 4px base in 4 / 8 / 16 multiples, opening to large editorial steps — 88px between bands, 120px on hero and the closing plan section.

08 — Foundations

Border Radius

Roundness is tiered by component: pills for buttons, 20px for cards, 12px for inputs, 28px for product-mockup chrome.

09 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

There is no drop-shadow language. Depth is built from canvas colour-blocking and surface-luminance shifts only.

Level 0 — flat. Default colour band, light or dark. No border, no shadow.
Level 1 — white card on a #f4f4f4 band, defined by a 1px #e2e2e7 hairline, not a shadow.
Level 2 — #16181a surface lifted off the #000000 canvas by luminance alone.
