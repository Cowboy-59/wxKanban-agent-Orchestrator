Design System
Analysis of Bang & Olufsen

A near-monochrome luxury system on white and warm cream, set in the BeoSupreme typeface at airy light weights, with a single deep forest green as its restrained accent — depth comes from photography and whitespace, never from shadow.

Tokens extracted live from bang-olufsen.com. Headline font is Lexend Deca — the brand's own declared fallback for its proprietary BeoSupreme typeface.

02 — Foundations

Typography

Everything is set in BeoSupreme (substituted here with Lexend Deca). The signature move is weight — headings stay at regular 400, never bold, so hierarchy is carried by size and tracking. Eyebrows are small but widely letter-spaced and uppercased.

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

Built on an 8px base unit (button padding 8 × 32px), scaling through a calm 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 ladder that keeps the layout airy.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

Rounding is bimodal: barely-there 3–5px on inputs and utility controls, or fully pill-shaped (40px) for primary and outline CTAs, with circles for icons.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

The headline of the whole system: Bang & Olufsen uses no drop shadows at all. Separation is achieved with hairlines, whitespace, and the depth of full-bleed imagery.

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. The default for nearly every surface.
Level 1 — still no shadow; a slightly stronger hairline marks a grouped panel.
Level 2 — the heaviest treatment is a solid 1px ink border, reserved for the most prominent containers.

Responsive Behavior

Across every breakpoint the priority is whitespace and imagery over density — the airy, uncluttered character is what survives, not a particular column count.
