Design System
Analysis of Allbirds

A near-monochrome, flat retail system where warm oatmeal beige is the canvas and near-black charcoal does all the talking — humanist Geograph type, fully-pilled buttons, hairline borders instead of shadows, and not a single gradient in sight.

02 — Foundations

Typography

Set in Geograph, a proprietary humanist geometric sans — shown here in its nearest Google substitute, Hanken Grotesk. Hierarchy is built through size and weight (tracking stays normal). A 14px / 400 tier carries almost all body copy; a heavy 900 weight is reserved for short uppercase labels.

04 — Components

Cards & Surfaces

Product cards are borderless: a softly-rounded beige media tile, then ink title and price below. Depth is absent — a faint lift appears only on hover and on floating sale badges.

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

Built on a 4px base unit (8px effective rhythm). Control padding clusters around 12–13px vertical and 24–25px horizontal.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

Soft and fully rounded throughout — from a 3px detail up to 80px media corners and a 100px pill. Buttons and inputs are true pills.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. The brand default. Surfaces are separated by borders and the beige/white contrast, not shadow.
Level 1 — a barely-there 1px lift, used only on floating sale badges and on card hover.
Level 2 — a soft 24px-blur drop for the cart drawer, modals and dialogs. The only place depth gets real.
09 — Responsive

Responsive Behavior

NameWidthKey changes
Mobile< 768pxSingle / two-up product grid, hamburger nav, full-width pill CTAs, stacked beige bands.
Tablet768–1023pxTwo-up grids, condensed header, side padding tightens to ~22px.
Laptop1024–1279pxFour-up product grids return, full horizontal nav.
Desktop≥ 1280pxCentred 1200px column with generous gutters; editorial full-bleed beige sections.

Graceful by design: because the system leans on so few moving parts — pills, hairlines and beige bands — it collapses cleanly across breakpoints with almost no visual change.
