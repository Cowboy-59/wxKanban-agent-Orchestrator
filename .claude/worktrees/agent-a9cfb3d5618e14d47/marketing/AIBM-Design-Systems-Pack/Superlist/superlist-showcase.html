Superlist · Design System

Tasks, notes, and plans.
Finally in one place.

A confident dark-first productivity brand — near-black indigo, crisp white grotesque type, and a playful spectrum of periwinkle, pink and a coral-to-red CTA gradient.

02 — Foundations

Typography

Headlines in Haffer XH (proprietary) — shown here in its Google substitute, Space Grotesk. Body copy in Inter, the site's genuine body face. Hierarchy is built from size and negative tracking, not weight.

03 — Components

Button Variants

button-cta (gradient)
The hero call-to-action. A warm vertical red→coral gradient with a soft layered shadow — the one warm note in a cool palette.
button-periwinkle
Solid periwinkle brand fill (#7577e0) for in-product primary actions.
button-dark
Indigo-black fill matching the dark canvas, for high-contrast actions on light sections.
button-secondary
Outlined on a 1px ink border — quiet companion to the CTA.
link · inline
FeaturesUse Cases

04 — Components

Cards & Surfaces

Cards use a generous 20px radius and sit flat until hover, when they lift on a soft 10px/15px shadow. Vivid purple→pink fills stand in for Superlist's illustrative thumbnails.

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

An 8px base unit drives a 4–48px scale, with generous 40px side gutters around a centred 1200px column.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

Rounding is generous and tiered — 8px on controls, a prominent 20px on cards, 100px pills, and a dramatic 200px "arch" on illustration frames.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. The default resting state for cards and sections.
Level 1 — soft 0 10px 15px rgba(0,0,0,.08) card-hover lift.
Level 2 — deeper 0 24px 48px rgba(24,24,36,.18) for the floating CTA and modals.
09 — Responsive

Responsive Behavior

NameWidthKey changes
Mobile< 768pxSingle-column grids; hero display drops to ~48px; gutters tighten to 22px.
Tablet768–1023px4-up card grids collapse to 2-up; pricing and auth stack to one column.
Laptop1024–1279pxFull multi-column grids return; hero scales back up toward full display size.
Desktop≥ 1280pxCentred 1200px column with 40px gutters; full alternating dark/light bands.

The page is strongly sectioned, alternating dark indigo and lavender-white full-bleed bands with a consistent vertical rhythm of ~72px.
