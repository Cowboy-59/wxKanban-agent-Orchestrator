Design System
Analysis of Mux

A flat, high-contrast developer brand on a paper-white canvas and charcoal bands, where a hot orange-to-amber CTA and a confetti of pink, green and blue accents bring play to an engineering-precise system.

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

A 7px-derived doubling rhythm — 7 / 14 / 28 / 56 / 112px — that mirrors the radius tiers and gives layouts their blocky, modular feel.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

Confidently round, from a barely-there 2px up to a 112px button pill and a full 9999px on chips and icon controls.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. The default card edge across the whole system.
Level 1 — still a 1px hairline. Mux uses no box-shadows; separation is structural, not floating.
Level 2 — emphasis comes from a solid charcoal (#242628) fill band rather than a shadow.
09 — Responsive

Responsive Behavior

NameWidthKey changes
Mobile< 768pxGrids collapse to a single column; display headline steps down to ~42px.
Tablet768–1023pxFour-up card grids become two-up; pricing and auth stack.
Laptop1024–1279pxFull four-column grids return; centred 1200px column with gutters.
Desktop≥ 1280pxMaximum width holds; charcoal/white banding stays the organising structure.

Note: The charcoal-and-white horizontal banding is the constant at every breakpoint — content reflows inside the bands rather than the band structure changing.
