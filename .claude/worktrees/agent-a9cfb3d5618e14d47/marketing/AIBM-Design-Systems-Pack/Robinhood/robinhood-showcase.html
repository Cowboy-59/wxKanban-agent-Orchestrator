Design System
Analysis of Robinhood

A confident, flat, dark-first system where acid-lime detonates against warm near-black and parchment neutrals — one proprietary grotesque held at weight 400, set with aggressive negative tracking, on full-pill buttons and shadowless surfaces.

Type rendered in Hanken Grotesk as the nearest-Google substitute for Robinhood's proprietary Capsule Sans Text.

Typography

Hierarchy is built from size and negative tracking, not weight — nearly every tier sits at 400. Display tiers tighten to -1 to -1.5px; small labels track tighter still.

07 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

A 4 / 8 base, with 16px and 32px doing most of the work — note the 0 32px padding on every pill button.

08 — Foundations

Border Radius

The page is dominated by the 36px pill applied to all buttons; minor insets use 3–5px and larger cards round to 20px.

09 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. The brand default: Robinhood is essentially flat and uses no shadows in its page UI.
Level 1 — a barely-there 1px lift, reserved for the rare overlay or floating control.
Level 2 — a deeper drop for modals/popovers only; off-brand for the marketing surfaces.
10 — Responsive

Responsive Behavior

NameWidthKey changes
Mobile< 768pxBands collapse to a single column; display type steps down toward ~48px; pill CTAs stretch toward full width.
Tablet768–1023pxMulti-column tiles reduce to two columns; hero headline reduces; section padding tightens.
Laptop1024–1279pxFull multi-column grids return within the centered max-width column.
Desktop≥ 1280pxContent caps at ~1200px and centers; full-bleed color bands run edge to edge behind it.

Layout logic: a wide centered column of full-bleed, alternating-tone bands — black, then parchment or white — stacked vertically, with the lime accent appearing only on CTAs and key marks.
