Design System
Analysis of Glossier

A near-monochrome editorial beauty system — white canvas, black Apercu type set small and light, one electric blue and a whisper of pink, on shapes kept square and flat.

02 — Foundations

Typography

Apercu, Glossier's proprietary neo-grotesque — substituted here with Hanken Grotesk. Hierarchy is built almost entirely through size, in a narrow 12–28px band, with weight rarely above regular.

03 — Components

04 — Components

Cards & Surfaces

Product cards are borderless and flat — separated by whitespace, not shadow. Imagery sits in soft-pink-to-grey tiles, with type kept small beneath.

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

A roughly 8px base with generous large steps (40px, 64px) that create the open, gallery-like rhythm Glossier is known for.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

Square by design. Radii barely exist — a 2–3px hint on cards and inputs, zero on primary buttons, with a 50px pill kept for small badges only.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. The default: cards and sections separate through whitespace, not shadow.
Level 1 — a very soft, low-opacity lift used on card hover.
Level 2 — a faint glow reserved for dropdowns and overlays. Nothing reads as strongly raised.
09 — Responsive

Responsive Behavior

NameWidthKey changes
Mobile< 768pxSingle-column grid, hero stacks, slim uppercase nav collapses to a menu toggle.
Tablet768–1023pxProduct grids drop to 2-up; generous side padding preserved.
Laptop1024–1279pxFull 3–4-up gallery grids return; whitespace rhythm widens.
Desktop≥ 1280pxMax 1200px content frame, centered, with the type and chrome unchanged.

Because the type is already small and the chrome already sparse, the mobile collapse is gentle — the visual language stays black-on-white, flat, and square at every width.
