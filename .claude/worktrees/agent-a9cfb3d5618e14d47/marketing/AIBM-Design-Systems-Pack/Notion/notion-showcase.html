Design System
Analysis of Notion

A warm, near-monochrome workspace aesthetic — paper-white surfaces over a soft oatmeal canvas, near-black warm ink, and a single confident blue carrying every CTA and link, with a playful saturated accent set kept strictly inside the product illustrations.

01 — Foundations

Colour Palette

A warm greyscale doing nearly all the work, spiked by one decisive action blue. The saturated accents below are an illustration palette — they never appear as interface chrome.

02 — Foundations

Typography

NotionInter — a custom cut of Inter — shown here via Google Fonts Inter, the nearest faithful substitute. Hierarchy is built through size and tight negative tracking; weight stays within 400 / 500 / 700.

03 — Components

Button Variants

button-primary
Solid action blue (#0075de), white text, 8px radius — the single highest-emphasis control.
button-dark
Pure-black fill for secondary emphasis and app-download moments on light sections.
button-secondary
Outlined variant — a 1px ink border on white, or a light-blue tinted fill with blue text.
button-pill
Full-radius chip used for "what's new" tags, filters, and toggles.
link · inline
TemplatesPricing
Inline links inherit ink and underline; action links switch to the brand blue.
04 — Components

Cards & Surfaces

Cards rest flat on a hairline border and lift with a soft, multi-layer shadow on hover. Radius is 12px, with a media tile filling the top.

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

A 4px base unit expands through a 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 scale that maps cleanly onto the type rhythm.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

8px is the default for buttons and inputs. The system tops out at 16px plus true pills — nothing bubbly.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. The resting state for almost every card.
Level 1 — a four-layer soft shadow (1–4% black) topping out at 4px / 18px blur. Used on hover.
Level 2 — a six-layer diffuse shadow fanning out to 36px / 89px for popovers and modals.
09 — Responsive

Responsive Behavior

NameWidthKey changes
Mobile< 768pxSingle column; hero display drops to ~48px; nav collapses to a menu.
Tablet768–1023pxFour-column card rows fold to two; gutters tighten.
Laptop1024–1279pxFull multi-column grid returns within the ~1200px column.
Desktop≥ 1280pxCentred column with wide outer margins; display type at full scale.

The layout trusts whitespace and faint top-rules — not heavy bands — to separate sections, keeping the premium, document-first feel at every width.
