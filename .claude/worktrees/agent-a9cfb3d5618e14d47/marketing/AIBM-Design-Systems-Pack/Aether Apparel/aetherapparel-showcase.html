Design System
Analysis of AETHER Apparel

A stark, gallery-white system with absolute-black ink, no saturated colour, and a single warm sand accent — built for technical apparel, with all the personality carried by an extended grotesque and a utilitarian mono.

01 — Foundations

Colour Palette

Effectively monochrome: black ink, white canvas, one soft grey, and a single warm sand. Contrast does the work a palette usually would.

02 — Foundations

Typography

Three Maison Neue cuts — substituted here with Archivo (expanded), Inter, and IBM Plex Mono. Hierarchy is built from size, not weight; the brand voice lives in the tiny mono.

03 — Components

04 — Components

Product Cards

Flat by default — a 5px-radius image on white, separated by hairlines. A soft even glow appears only on hover. Prices are set in mono.

05 — Components

Form Elements

Minimal fields on the 5px radius, hairline border darkening to ink on focus. Labels are set in uppercase mono.

text-input · email
textarea · message
06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

A 4px base on an 8px working rhythm, scaling out to generous 48–64px gaps that let full-width imagery breathe.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

A barely-rounded 5px workhorse for media and inputs; controls round all the way to a pill.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. The default. Nearly everything sits flat on the canvas.
Level 1 — a soft, even 0 0 8px rgba(0,0,0,.2) glow, the one shadow used in the wild, appearing on hover.
Level 2 — a deeper, diffuse drop reserved for the cart drawer and modal overlays.
