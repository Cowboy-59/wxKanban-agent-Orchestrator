Design System
Analysis of Discord

A deep-indigo canvas lit by electric blurple and a fresh mint-green CTA — where ultra-tight uppercase display type slams against pure-white rounded surfaces with black ink. Playful, loud, and unmistakably game-native.

Typography

Ggsans is Discord's proprietary humanist sans — shown here with Inter as the body substitute and Archivo standing in for the heavy uppercase display face. Hierarchy is built on size, weight, and aggressively tight line-height rather than tracking.

04 — Components

Cards & Surfaces

Discord separates content by raw colour contrast — white panels and vivid blurple-to-indigo image masks on the dark canvas — rather than by shadow. Corners are a strong 16px.

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

A loose 8px base with generous 40px and 64px steps that give the full-bleed marketing bands their airy rhythm.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

Roundness is the dominant texture. A 16px radius shows up everywhere; oversized 30–56px radii shape hero panels and image masks; pills go fully round.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. The default. Discord's live site is almost entirely flat; separation comes from colour, not shadow.
Level 1 — a barely-there 1px drop used on hover for cards and floating pills.
Level 2 — a deeper soft shadow reserved for modals and overlays in the product UI.
09 — Responsive

Responsive Behavior

NameWidthKey changes
Mobile< 768pxCard grids collapse to one or two columns; display type drops to ~40px; coloured bands stack vertically keeping rounded white panels.
Tablet768–1023pxFour-up grids become two-up; hero padding tightens; split panels stack.
Laptop1024–1279pxFull multi-column layout returns; hero art sits beside copy.
Desktop≥ 1280pxMax content width ~1200px, centered, with edge-to-edge coloured bands beyond it.

Layout note: a content-light, image-and-headline-heavy system that prioritises brand energy and colour over information density.
