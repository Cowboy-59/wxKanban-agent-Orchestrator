Design System
Analysis of Anthropic

A warm, editorial system painted on cream with near-black ink, where a single signature clay-orange carries the brand voice and an unusual serif-led body gives the whole site a calm, bookish, paper-like character.

02 — Foundations

Typography

Three proprietary families — Anthropic Serif, Sans, and Mono — substituted here with their nearest Google equivalents (Newsreader, Inter, IBM Plex Mono). The defining choice is serif body copy: hierarchy is built with size and tracking in the serif, and with weight in the sans.

04 — Components

Cards & Surfaces

Surfaces are flat oat and sand tints separated by hairlines, not shadows. Cards round to 16px and lift only on hover with a faint, finely layered shadow.

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

A 4px base unit with a calm 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 rhythm — buttons pad to 8×16, sections breathe with generous vertical space.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

Corners stay tight. A 4px workhorse on buttons and chrome, stepping up through 8–24px for cards and panels, and full-pill for badges.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. The default: flat surfaces separated by tone, never a shadow.
Level 1 — a finely layered, 1–4% black stack (2px / 4px / 16px). Lift without weight, used on hover.
Level 2 — a deeper version for floating panels and modals, still soft and low-contrast.
09 — Responsive

Responsive Behavior

NameWidthKey changes
Mobile< 768pxSingle column; card & listing grids collapse to one, oversized editorial headline drops to display size, gutters tighten to ~22px.
Tablet768–1023pxFour-up grids become two-up; pricing and auth stack to one column; nav condenses.
Laptop1024–1279pxFull four-column grids return; centered ~1200px column with 40px gutters.
Desktop≥ 1280pxMax column width holds; surrounding whitespace grows rather than the content.

Layout note: content is held in a centered ~1200px column with roomy vertical whitespace; sections are divided by tone and hairline rather than by heavy framing, keeping the paper-first character intact at every breakpoint.
