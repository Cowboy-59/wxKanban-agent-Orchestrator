Design System
Analysis of Stripe

Near-black indigo ink on an airy off-white canvas, lit by a single electric blurple (#635BFF) and a cyan-to-mint brand gradient, typeset in the proprietary Söhne grotesque — tiny radii and soft slate-tinted shadows give every surface a precise, engineered calm.

04 — Components

Cards & Surfaces

Marketing surfaces are white “bento” product cards on the off-white wash, with 16–24px radii and a soft slate-tinted shadow that lifts on hover.

06 — Foundations

Spacing Scale

An 8px base unit (with a 4px half-step), opening to ~80px for the vertical rhythm between marketing sections.

07 — Foundations

Border Radius

Small by default — inputs at 8px, buttons and cards 16–24px, pills and badges fully rounded.

08 — Foundations

Elevation & Depth

Level 0 — 1px hairline only. Flat surfaces and form fields rest here.
Level 1 — the signature slate-tinted card lift, used on hover.
Level 2 — deeper stack for modals, menus, and popovers.
09 — Responsive

Responsive Behavior

NameWidthKey changes
Mobile< 768pxBento grids stack to one column; the 64px display drops to ~40px; nav collapses to a menu.
Tablet768–1023pxFour-up product grids become two-up; container gutters tighten.
Laptop1024–1279pxFull multi-column bento grids appear inside a ~1080px centred container.
Desktop≥ 1280pxMax ~1200px container with wide whitespace gutters; gradient hero art at full bleed.

Whitespace is preserved aggressively at every breakpoint so the airy, engineered calm survives the collapse.
