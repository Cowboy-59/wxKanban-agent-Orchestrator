## 1. Facebook group — primary launch post (Andy's own WINDEV group)

After 40+ years building software, I finally did the thing a lot of us have been quietly worrying about.

I took one of my own WINDEV applications — HFSQL, windows, global procedures, the whole thing — and converted it into a modern, AI-built full-stack app running on an open database I actually own. Postgres in this case, but it could just as easily be MS SQL or MySQL.

I'm not posting this to stir anything up. I love what we've built over the years. But we all know what changed: the licensing moved to subscription, and now our source code itself is something we rent. Miss a payment and you can lose access to the very apps we — and our clients — depend on. I've watched this exact thing happen to two other development environments in my career. I'm not going to pretend it isn't happening here.

The difference now is AI. For the first time, there's a real, guided way across — without throwing away the decades of judgment we've each earned. The lifecycle skills you already have are exactly what this new world runs on.

I built a tool called **wxKanban** to make that crossing — and here's the part that matters: wxKanban *itself* is the WINDEV app I converted. The proof is the product.

It releases **July 5th**. If you've been quietly worrying about the same thing, I'd love for you to come see how the crossing actually works — the whole approach, the same one I used on my own app.

Here's where to read more and sign up to follow it to launch: **https://windev.wxperts.com/**

Happy to answer any questions right here in the comments too. 🐎

— Andy "Cowboy" Stapleton
