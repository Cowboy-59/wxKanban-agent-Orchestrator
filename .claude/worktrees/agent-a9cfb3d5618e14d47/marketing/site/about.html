About

The bridge to software you actually own.

wxKanban is a guided crossing from proprietary, vendor-locked development platforms to modern, AI-built full-stack software — your data, your code, your foundations.

What we do

For decades, skilled developers have built real businesses on closed platforms. That worked — until the terms changed. Perpetual licenses became subscriptions, source code became something you rent, and AI began rewriting how software gets built. wxKanban exists to get you across that gap safely: we convert your existing application — database, business logic, and screens — onto a modern stack on open foundations, with AI doing the heavy lifting while you keep the judgment, the control, and the ownership.

Why it exists

We've watched this happen before — more than once, capable people built their careers on a development environment, then found themselves trapped by it. The way out was never about memorizing the newest syntax. It was about understanding how software actually gets built, and carrying that judgment from one platform to the next. That skill — the full lifecycle, from messy reality to a clean, tested, shipped result — is exactly what AI-assisted development still demands. wxKanban is built to honor the experience you already have and hand you the new tools to use it.

The proof is the product

wxKanban isn't a theory about converting off a proprietary platform. It is one. This product began life as a WINDEV application — we converted it, data, procedures, screens, and all, into a modern AI-built full-stack app using the very method we offer you. If wxKanban could make it across, so can your software.

More than a tool — a way of working

The other side of the bridge isn't an empty stack. wxKanban is a spec-driven development environment: a guided six-phase lifecycle — Design → Implementation → QA → Human Testing → Beta → Release — where nothing ships without an approved spec, tests, and sign-off, so AI never runs away with your project. Just as importantly, it keeps your specs and documentation consistent as the software evolves, so future changes update the record instead of drifting out of date.

And it gives you what you need to run real work as a team:

  • Team management — invite people, set their roles, and control who can do what.
  • Check-out & check-in — claim a scope to work on it and check it back in when you're done, so work stays coordinated and no one quietly overwrites someone else.
  • The Cockpit — a live command center for your project: where it stands, what's next, and every wxKanban command in one place.
  • Active, in-context help — guidance and answers right where you work, both inside wxKanban and inside VS Code.

You learn to build like a professional, not just to prompt — whether you're a twenty-year veteran or just starting out.

Who's behind it
