Per the research: in **late 2024 PCSoft was acquired by Constellation Software (Canada)**,
and in **2025 ended perpetual/dongle licenses, forcing a SaaS subscription** — under which
**developers reportedly lose access to their own source code if they stop paying**. This
triggered backlash and the formation of the **Wx Alliance** association explicitly to fight
vendor lock-in.

If accurate, this means the campaign's core fear ("what happens the day they pull the plug?")
is **not hypothetical — it's happening right now**, and the audience is already organized and
emotionally activated. Lead messaging with it. **Andy: confirm these facts before we put them
in public copy.**
