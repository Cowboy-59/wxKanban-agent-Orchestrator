# Registration #

## general information ##

Registration consists of 2 things, 1 registration of the company information , choice of payment , and contact information for administration.

Choice of payment consists of
FREE -- small Company  1 to 3 person
Small/Med Business -- single Company no more than  5 users SOC 2 and HIGHTRUST.
Consultant -- 19.95 per person Single company - allows multiple connections to outside management systems.
Enterprise -- 59.95 Single Company -- 25 users + 5.00 per additional user after.

once the company information has been added, the contact info is also inserted into the userprofile table with responsibility of "ADMIN"

once they have chosen payment option get payment information for stripe if not free version.then take the admin to the admin Dashboard.

admin dashboard will show number of projects active, number of tasks in progress, personnel of team members 

allow the admin to put in any additional personnel that will add an invitation record for each email address and send via email.

research stripe cli for setup of subscription payment.
