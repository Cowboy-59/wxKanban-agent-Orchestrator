# FAQ

The FAQ is a quick-answer library for common questions about using wxKanban. Come here for short, plain answers — and if yours isn't listed, you can ask, and it gets added. For a full, area-by-area reference of what each feature does and what your plan includes, see [Help](help.md) instead.

## What you're looking at

A heading — "Browse answers, or ask a question and we'll add it to the FAQ" — sits above a list of **Frequently asked questions**. Each question is a row you click to expand and read its answer. Typical entries include:

- *How do I start a new project in wxKanban?*
- *What is the difference between a Scope and a Spec?*
- *What is the spec-first rule in wxKanban?*
- *What is the Orchestrator Kit in wxKanban?*
- *What is the VS Code Cockpit?*
- *How does wxKanban support AI-assisted development?*
- *What are the billing and pricing plans for wxKanban?*
- *What kind of support does wxKanban offer?*

## Asking a question

If the answer you need isn't there, use the ask box to submit your question. It's routed to the wxKanban team and, once answered, added to the FAQ so the next person benefits too.

## Common tasks

1. **Find an answer:** scan the list and click a question to expand it.
2. **Ask something new:** type your question in the ask box and submit it.
3. **Understand a whole feature area:** open [Help](help.md) for the fuller reference.

## Fields and what they show

- **Question rows** — click to expand and reveal the answer.
- **Ask box** — submits a new question to the wxKanban team for inclusion.
