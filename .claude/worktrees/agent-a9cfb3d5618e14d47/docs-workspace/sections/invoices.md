# Invoices

The Invoices screen turns billable time into client invoices, and keeps a history of everything you've billed. It draws on the billable time your team logs, so you generate an invoice instead of assembling one by hand.

> **Plan note:** Invoicing is a **Consultant plan** feature. On Small Business the sidebar link shows a padlock; open [Help](help.md) to compare plans or upgrade from the [Dashboard](dashboard.md).

## What you're looking at

A **Generate Invoice** button sits at the top right. Three summary cards show **Total Invoices**, **Total Revenue**, and **Sent Invoices**. Below is the **Invoice History** list. When there are no invoices yet, it prompts you to create your first invoice.

## Generating an invoice

Click **Generate Invoice** to build one from billable time. wxKanban gathers the billable time entries for a project and date range, multiplies the hours by the hourly rate, and produces a PDF invoice. Once an entry is billed it's marked as invoiced so it can't be billed twice. The new invoice then appears in the history with its own status (e.g. draft, sent, paid).

The **Invoice-Ready Billable Preview** on the [Work Hub](work-hub.md) is the companion view — it shows what's available to bill before you generate anything.

## Common tasks

1. **Create an invoice:** click **Generate Invoice**, pick the project and date range, and generate the PDF.
2. **Track billing at a glance:** read the **Total Invoices**, **Total Revenue**, and **Sent Invoices** cards.
3. **Find a past invoice:** scroll the **Invoice History** list.

## Fields and what they show

- **Total Invoices** — how many invoices you've created.
- **Total Revenue** — the summed value of your invoices.
- **Sent Invoices** — how many have been sent to clients.
- **Invoice History** — each invoice with its client, amount, and status.
- **Generate Invoice** — creates a new invoice from billable time entries.
