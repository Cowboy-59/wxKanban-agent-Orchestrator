# Settings

Settings is where an administrator manages company-wide details and the subscription: your company's name and billing contacts, your plan, and payment. All settings are encrypted at rest.

## What you're looking at

The page is titled **Company Settings** with the note "Manage application-wide settings for your company. All settings are encrypted at rest." It has two main areas: **Company Information** and **Billing & Subscription**.

## Company Information

A form for your company's core details:

- **Company Name** — your organization's name, shown throughout the app as the active tenant.
- **Billing Email** — the general contact email for the company.
- **Consultant Invoice Email** — where business-to-business (B2B) invoices are delivered; leave blank if not applicable.
- **Billing Address** — your company's postal billing address.
- **Billing Phone** — a contact phone number.

Click **Save Company Info** to store changes.

## Billing & Subscription

The **Subscription & Payment** panel shows your current **Plan** (e.g. SMALL_BUSINESS) and its **Status** (e.g. ACTIVE), your Stripe subscription reference, and controls to review your plan and update your card if a payment failed. This is the place to fix a failed payment or add a card. To change tiers, use the upgrade path (the **Upgrade Plan** buttons on the [Dashboard](dashboard.md), or compare options in [Help](help.md)).

## Common tasks

1. **Update your company details:** edit the **Company Information** fields and click **Save Company Info**.
2. **Set where invoices are emailed:** fill in **Consultant Invoice Email**.
3. **Fix a failed payment / update your card:** use the **Subscription & Payment** panel.
4. **Check your plan and status:** read the **Plan** and **Status** in Billing & Subscription.

## Fields and what they show

- **Company Name** — your organization's name (the active tenant label).
- **Billing Email** — general company contact address.
- **Consultant Invoice Email** — destination for B2B invoices.
- **Billing Address / Billing Phone** — postal and phone billing contacts.
- **Plan** — your current subscription tier.
- **Status** — subscription state (e.g. ACTIVE).
- **Subscription & Payment** — review plan and update payment method.
