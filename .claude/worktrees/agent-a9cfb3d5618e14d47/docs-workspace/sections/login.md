# Signing in

The sign-in screen is where you enter wxKanban. Come here at the start of a session, or any time you've been signed out. You'll need the email address and password for your wxKanban account — the same account your company administrator created or invited you to.

## What you're looking at

1. **Email Address** — type the email you registered with (or were invited on). This is how wxKanban finds your account and your company.
2. **Password** — type your password. The small eye icon on the right toggles the password between hidden dots and plain text so you can check what you typed.
3. **Sign In** — submits your credentials. On success you land on your home screen: the [Dashboard](dashboard.md) if you're an admin, or [My Tasks](tasks.md) if you're a team member. If the email or password is wrong you'll see an error and stay on this page; after several failed attempts the account is temporarily locked for security.
4. **Register here** — for brand-new companies only. It opens the sign-up wizard to create a new company account. If your company already exists, do **not** register again — ask your admin to invite you instead (see [Team Members](team.md)).

## Forgot your password?

Click **Forgot password?** below the Sign In button. You'll be asked for your email and wxKanban sends a reset link. The link is time-limited, so use it promptly.

## Connect a PM system to get started

At the bottom, the **Jira / Monday / Asana / Trello** chips are shortcuts for connecting a project-management tool as a first step. Most users sign in normally first and connect these later from [PM Integrations](integrations.md); the chips are here mainly for new users who want to start by linking an external task source.

## Common tasks

1. **Sign in:** enter email → enter password → click **Sign In**.
2. **Check a mistyped password:** click the eye icon in the password field to reveal it.
3. **Reset a forgotten password:** click **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and follow the emailed link.

## Fields and what they show

- **Email Address** — the account identifier; must match an existing wxKanban user.
- **Password** — your secret; never shared. The eye icon only toggles local visibility, it is not sent anywhere.
- **Sign In** — authenticates and starts a session that lasts up to 24 hours before you'll need to sign in again.
- **Forgot password?** — starts the email-based password reset flow.
- **Register here** — creates a **new** company account; not for joining an existing company.
