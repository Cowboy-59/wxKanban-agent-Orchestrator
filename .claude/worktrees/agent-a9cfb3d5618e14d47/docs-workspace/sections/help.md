# Help & plan features

The Help page is a complete, area-by-area reference to what every part of wxKanban does — and, crucially, **which plan includes it**. Come here to understand a feature before using it, or to see what you'd gain by upgrading. It's available on every plan (the life-buoy icon in the sidebar). For short answers to specific questions, use the [FAQ](faq.md).

## What you're looking at

At the top are the four **public plans** as selectable cards — **Single User** ($19.95/mo), **Small/Medium Business** ($29.95/mo), **Consultant** ($39.95/mo), and **Enterprise** ($59.95/mo) — each with its price and headline limit (users). Your current plan is marked **YOUR PLAN** and selected by default. A legend shows the two states: 🟢 **Included** and 🟠 **Upgrade required**.

Below, features are grouped by area — **Core Workspace**, **Development Lifecycle**, and more — with one card per feature. Each card names the feature, explains what it does, gives a short **How to:** tip, and shows whether it's **Included** or **Upgrade required** for the plan selected at the top.

## Comparing plans

Click a different plan card at the top and every feature card updates its **Included / Upgrade required** badge to reflect that plan. This is the fastest way to answer "what would I get on Consultant?" — for example, **Time Tracking & Billing, Invoices, and Reports** are included from the **Consultant** plan up, which is why they show a padlock on Small Business.

## Feature areas you'll find here

- **Core Workspace** — [Dashboard](dashboard.md), [Projects](projects.md), [My Tasks](tasks.md), [Settings](settings.md), [FAQ](faq.md).
- **Development Lifecycle** — the 6-phase lifecycle & orchestrator, specs & scopes, audit reports, and related tooling.
- …and further groups covering integrations, billing, and administration.

## Common tasks

1. **Learn what a feature does:** find its card and read the description and **How to:** tip.
2. **See if your plan includes something:** check the badge on its card (your plan is selected by default).
3. **Compare with another plan:** click that plan's card at the top and watch the badges change.

## Fields and what they show

- **Plan cards (Single User / Small-Medium Business / Consultant / Enterprise)** — price, user limit, and which one is **YOUR PLAN**; selecting one sets the comparison.
- **Included 🟢 / Upgrade required 🟠** — whether the selected plan includes that feature.
- **Feature card** — a description plus a **How to:** tip for each app area.
